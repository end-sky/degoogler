official vs community builds — what to expect
  • The official tag means a build went through a comprehensive testing cycle and can be used as a daily driver.
  • community builds carry the latest development code. They are provided as best effort, without any guarantee, and may include known and significant bugs.
  • Unofficial builds are made by community members — use them at your own risk.
  • Custom builds are made by users and may include the builder's own modifications — use them at your own risk.
Both community and official builds are delivered OTA (Over The Air) to supported devices.
